About

Careers and workforces, built carefully.

Skills Solutions Australia supports graduates and job seekers with internships, career coaching and application support, and helps employers with recruitment and labour hire. We are based in Hobart and work Australia-wide.

Director

Julia Lane

With a decade of experience in the education and ICT sectors, Julia is passionate about empowering the next generation of professionals and connecting them with meaningful career opportunities.

She previously served as State Operations Manager at the Australian College of Applied Professions, leading the Professional Year Program and managing operations across multiple states, so that each graduate had a clear career development path.

Her expertise lies in designing programs that address the gap between recent graduates and industry needs, combining tailored support with practical internships. She has a track record in recruitment and talent management, including using LinkedIn to attract and engage talent.

Julia’s leadership style is respectful, inclusive and dedicated to the growth of the people she works with. Her mission is to improve workforce readiness and create pathways for talented people to thrive in their chosen careers.
