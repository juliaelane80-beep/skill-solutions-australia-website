Who This Program Is For

The Career Accelerator program may suit you if you are:

Applying for jobs but not getting interviews

Find out what's holding your applications back and how to make them more competitive.

New to Tasmania or Australia

Understand the local job market and how to position your background for Australian employers.

An ESL job seeker

Get patient, practical coaching to communicate your strengths clearly in writing and at interview.

Unsure how to present your experience

Learn how to translate your background into language and structure that resonates with employers.

Lacking confidence in interviews

Practise with structured mock interviews and build the confidence to perform under pressure.

Wanting honest, practical career guidance

Receive straightforward advice from someone who knows what Tasmanian employers actually want.

What Makes This Different

This is more than just resume writing.

The Career Accelerator program focuses on helping you understand the Tasmanian employment market and improving how you present yourself professionally to employers.

With strong employer connections across Tasmania and extensive experience supporting graduates and job seekers into employment pathways, the support provided is practical, personalised, and focused on real outcomes.
